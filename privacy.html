Privacy notice

This notice describes what happens to data when you visit tech.aldurmc.com. It is written to match what the site actually does, and we update it when the site changes.

Who we are

Al Durr Al Manthour Advanced Business Solutions, Jeddah, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. For any question about this notice or your data, write to info@aldurmc.com.

What we collect on this site

Nothing directly. This site has no contact form, no account, no newsletter and no comment field. We do not ask you for your name, your email address or any other personal detail in order to read it.

What is stored in your browser

One entry, and only if you use the light and dark switch: a value named aldur-theme recording which grade you picked. It stays in your browser, it is never sent to us or anyone else, and clearing your browser data removes it. It is not an advertising or tracking cookie.

Third parties that receive data when you visit

  • Our hosting provider serves this site. Delivering a page to you means their servers receive your IP address and browser details, which they process for delivery, security and abuse prevention.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Your browser requests them from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, and Google receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Google Calendar handles booking. The "Book an intro meeting" button takes you to a Google appointment page. Anything you enter there — your name, email and chosen time — is collected by Google and reaches us as a calendar invitation. Google's handling of it is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

We do not sell data, we run no advertising, and we do not share your information with anyone beyond the services named above.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and our reply in our Google Workspace mailbox so we can carry on the conversation and keep a record of what was agreed.

Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, and under the GDPR if you are in the European Union, you may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. Write to info@aldurmc.com and we will respond.

Changes

When the site changes in a way that affects this notice, we update the notice and the date at the top of this page.

A note on this document. It is a plain description of how the website behaves, written by the people who built it. It is not legal advice, and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. If this site later collects personal data directly, that review should happen first.
