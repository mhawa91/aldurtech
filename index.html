Five problems we are called about

Growing businesses tend to arrive with the same list.

  • Technology spending with no clear control

    Licences, vendors and projects are bought separately and never add up.

  • Systems that do not talk to each other

    The same figure is keyed into three systems and reconciled by hand.

  • Vendor decisions made without an expert on your side

    Proposals are judged on price because nobody can judge the technical case.

  • Projects that go live but are not really used

    The system is delivered, the training is skipped and the old process survives.

  • No one accountable for IT as a whole

    Everyone owns a piece, so the gaps between the pieces belong to nobody.
